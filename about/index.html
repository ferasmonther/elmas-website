ABOUT ELMA’S

Rooted here. Looking ahead.

We’re a Lebanon-based agritech company focused on Black Soldier Fly larvae for chicken and fish feed applications, alongside complementary organic soil inputs.

OUR MISSION

A useful future
for organic resources.

Our mission is to provide sustainable, high-quality Black Soldier Fly larvae as a practical protein source for animal feed, while supporting organic resource recovery.

Alongside our core BSFL larvae product, we offer distinct soil input product lines: BSFL frass and vermicompost. Each has its own production process and potential applications.

OUR VISION

Growing with purpose.

We aspire to become a leading Black Soldier Fly farming business in Lebanon. Our aim is to scale responsibly, recover more organic material and support farmers with locally produced agricultural inputs.

Practical value, close to home.

We bring together biological conversion and hands-on agricultural work. Our focus is on developing useful products and understanding what customers need, from feed formulation to soil care.

Through careful production and continued learning, we aim to contribute to food security, circular agriculture and a more resourceful local economy.
